Google Tasks supports creating tasks and subtasks.

However, when using list_tasks, all tasks and subtasks are returned without revealing the hierarchy. For example:

```
Tasks in list NWlFTUw3T0FOdy11VFp6cg for REDACTED:
- Clean house (ID: eTRGTzlSeU9LUmdwU1hGWQ)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:45:38.051Z

- Vacuuming (ID: LTR4eVB6U20tOEJaOEJTSg)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:45:52.435Z

- Tidy (ID: UDZpM296SUxZNFMtLXR0Sw)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:45:55.652Z

- Dusting (ID: eTByajAzSTlzM2drN3pIeA)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:46:01.591Z

- Buy groceries (ID: VmViTFVPZDRUYWRWLXZpcQ)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:44:46.178Z

- Bananas (ID: Skh6THJlNzU3Smtpb2FfMw)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:44:52.899Z

- Broccoli (ID: aWEtUE5KTWpXODBTSUpFUg)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:44:58.057Z

- Ice cream (ID: VnFRSEx6NDNid09PdnA4Rg)
  Status: needsAction
  Updated: 2025-09-06T23:45:01.945Z
```

In theory, an agent could determine the hierarchy by calling get_task for each task and checking its parent field. However, users may have hundreds of tasks, necessitating hundreds of tool calls in order to fully understand that user's tasks.

**Describe the solution you'd like**
list_tasks should distinguish tasks and subtasks in the information that is returned.

Proposal:
1. Main tasks are output with a dash (-) bullet. (This is unchanged from current behavior.)
2. Subtasks are listed grouped below their main task. (This is unchanged from current behavior.)
3. Subtasks are output with an asterisk (*) bullet and are also indented two spaces. (This is the new behavior for this feature.)
